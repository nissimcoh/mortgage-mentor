# Calculator verification — October 18, 2026

## Implemented

- Amount parsing accepts safe whole-shekel integers, correctly grouped commas
  or spaces, and an optional ₪ prefix/suffix. Decimal, signed, malformed and
  unsafe integer inputs stay visible and fail validation. They are never
  silently converted into a different amount. Native digit edits can regroup
  an already valid number; arbitrary pasted text must pass the strict grammar.
- Formatting preserves the caret/selection during middle edits. Backspace
  across a comma moves left so the next backspace removes a digit.
- Amount, rate, term and reset-frequency errors have unique IDs and associated
  `aria-invalid`/`aria-describedby` attributes. Field names use separate labels,
  so error copy does not become part of the accessible field name. Invalid
  submission focuses the first invalid field, including repeat submissions;
  a calculation-level failure falls back to the focusable error summary.
- Makam forecasts now derive their model spread from the nominal 12-month
  zero yield, consistent with the captured bank calculation. The observed
  monthly Makam yield remains reference information, with its offered-minus-
  observed spread recorded separately. This distinction is explained in both
  languages. It is an empirical bank-calibration decision, not a claim that
  the bank's undisclosed contractual anchor equals our observed monthly yield.
- CPI-linked forecasts keep base payments at full internal precision and
  round the reported rows. This removes drift accumulated from rounding before
  indexing/repricing. Constant/no-forecast mode retains the ordinary fixed cash
  schedule convention; existing zero-inflation equivalence tests still pass.
- Calculator audit version increased from `1.1.0` to `1.2.0`. Existing saved
  snapshots keep their stored values/version. No stored rows are rewritten.
  Recalculation and a subsequent explicit save use the corrected engine. The
  existing version guard prevents reconstructing old comparison schedules as
  though they had been generated by the new methodology.

## Independent bank references

Source: [Leumi extended public calculator](https://ufapi.bankleumi.co.il/mortgageCalculator/ExtCalc).
Its own browser requests identify the public `productsCatalog` and
`CalculateByProducts` endpoints. Requests contain only synthetic mortgage
inputs; setting `returnPaymentDetails=true` returns complete per-loan schedules.
No account, loan application, contact details or bank credentials were used.

Eleven references were captured on October 18, 2026 at 09:58 UTC: ten forecast
cases and one fixed-unlinked control, totaling 2,724 monthly rows. Each uses
ILS 500,000, Spitzer and a 4.5% offered rate. Terms range from 20 to 25 years.
The app's recorded source is BOI `2026-09-calendar` (published October 5), BOI
rate 3.25%, and September Makam observation 3.1935502937%. The bank does not
disclose its curve ID; agreement is evidence for these cases, not proof of
the bank's internal data provenance.

| Case | Maximum monthly payment difference | Total payment difference (app − bank) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Unlinked government bond, 24/30/36/60/84/120-month resets | 0.00 each | 0.00 each |
| Annual Makam | 0.01 | +0.12 |
| Fixed CPI-linked | 0.01 | +0.01 |
| Variable CPI-linked, 60-month reset | 0.01 | +0.04 |
| Prime | 0.01 | −0.10 |
| Fixed unlinked control | 1.21, final month only | −1.21 |

The Makam correction changes the tested forecast total from ILS 826,972.68 to
819,211.44, versus the bank's 819,211.32. It removes an artificial observed-
yield/zero-curve basis spread; the first contractual year remains unchanged.

For all ten forecast cases, the current first payment and forecast maximum
match to the agora; displayed IRR matches to 0.01 percentage points. Every
monthly payment is compared, including every reset boundary. Principal and
interest components match within one agora except Prime, where the observed
maximum split difference is two agorot. Aggregate differences are recorded
above rather than described as exact matches.

The fixed-unlinked control exposes a legitimate modeling distinction: our
ordinary cash schedule debits the rounded amount and settles the remaining
balance in the last month. Leumi's forecast carries an unrounded base payment.
The first 239 payments match; our final payment is 3,162.04 versus 3,163.25.
That convention was preserved and tested explicitly, not hidden by relaxing
the forecast acceptance tolerance.

Methodology reference: [BOI Directive 451, supplement 4, pp. 23–24](https://www.boi.org.il/media/i5jbijyv/451_21.pdf).
Further bank/rate/term coverage and contract-specific rules remain listed in
[CALIBRATION_TODO.md](CALIBRATION_TODO.md). These samples do not establish
exhaustive or binding bank precision.
